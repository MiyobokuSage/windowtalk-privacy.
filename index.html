WindowTalk Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

WindowTalk is an Android application that uses device sensors to generate multilingual vocabulary and phrase candidates.

Information Collection

WindowTalk does not require account registration or personal information to operate. The current release is designed to operate offline and does not transmit user session data to the developer.

Device Sensors

WindowTalk uses available device sensors, including the accelerometer, magnetometer, and barometer, to detect changes in the device's environment. Sensor readings are processed locally on the device.

Session History

WindowTalk may store session history, accepted vocabulary candidates, timestamps, and associated definitions locally on the user's device. This information is used to provide session history and review features.

Data Sharing

WindowTalk does not intentionally transmit or sell personal information, sensor readings, or session history to third parties. Users may choose to export or share session data themselves.

Advertising and Analytics

The current release does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored sessions may be deleted using the app's session management features. Uninstalling the application ordinarily removes its locally stored application data, subject to Android device backup and restoration settings.

Children's Privacy

WindowTalk is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed toward children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if WindowTalk's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information provided on WindowTalk's Google Play store listing.
